FAQ

Questions, answered

Is BrainBuzz free?

Yes. The browser games on this site are free to play, with no ads and no sign-up. The BrainBuzz Android app is also free to download from Google Play.

Do my browser scores sync with the Android app?

No. Best scores you set here are saved only in this browser on this device. The Android app keeps its own progress on your phone, including XP, levels, streaks and achievements. The website and the app don’t share any data.

Are these the same games as in the app?

Yes. It’s the same seven games plus the Daily Challenge, with the same rules, difficulty levels and scoring. On any given date the Daily Challenge has the same 12 puzzles as the app.

What extra do I get in the Android app?

The app tracks your progress over time: XP and levels, daily streaks with a training calendar, three daily missions, 20 achievements, detailed stats and an optional daily reminder. In the app, Easy is always free and Medium and Hard unlock by watching a short optional video. On this website every level is free.

Can I play on iPhone, iPad or a computer?

Yes, in your browser. Every game on this site works on phones, tablets and computers, with touch, mouse or keyboard. The BrainBuzz app itself is available for Android on Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts on the website or in the app. The website saves your best scores in your browser’s local storage, and you can clear them at any time by clearing this site’s data.

Will brain games make me smarter?

BrainBuzz games are puzzles for enjoyment and practice. They are not a medical or scientific tool and aren't proven to raise IQ.
